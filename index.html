Who We Are

Welcome to Anuradha Coirs

ISO Certified 50+ Countries Export Quality

Anuradha Coirs is a trusted manufacturer and exporter of premium quality coconut-based products from India. With a strong commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction, we supply high-grade coir and coconut products to buyers across various international markets.

Located in the heart of South India’s coconut-growing region, Anuradha Coirs combines traditional expertise with modern processing methods to deliver eco-friendly and export-quality products suitable for agriculture, horticulture, hydroponics, industrial applications, and commercial trading.

Our wide range of products includes Coco Peat Blocks, Coco Peat Briquettes, Coir Fibre, Cocopeat Grow Bag Slab, Cocopeat Open Top Grow Bags, Coco Husk Chips, Coco Coins & Discs, Semi Husked Coconuts, Fully Husked Coconuts, Tender Coconuts, and Coconut Saplings. Every product is carefully processed, tested, and packed to meet international quality standards and buyer requirements.

Premium Export Quality

Every product batch passes rigorous laboratory quality checkups. We test for electrical conductivity (EC), moisture content, buffering standards, and expansion capacity to guarantee that every shipment meets premium international agricultural standards.

Competitive Pricing

Operating as a direct manufacturer with our own drying fields, processing machinery, and hydraulic balers allows us to bypass intermediaries. This direct supply chain passes structural cost savings straight to importers and nurseries worldwide.

Custom Packaging & OEM

We customize grow bag specifications, compressed block ratios, briquette designs, and polybag prints. Our private labeling support enables distributors and brands to configure retail-ready sizes according to target markets.

On-Time Delivery

With structured warehousing and proximity to major Southern Indian deep-water ports (Tuticorin and Cochin), we organize freight forwarding, secure vessel space, and complete container loading schedules with high reliability.

Eco-Friendly Products

Our coir fiber and peat substrates are 100% natural, biodegradable organic crop boosters. By reclaiming raw coconut husks, we support closed-loop farming and assist the global agricultural market in transitioning to carbon-neutral soil mediums.

Global Export Support

We navigate phytosanitary certification requirements, professional fumigation parameters, certificates of origin, and custom declarations. Our experience guarantees seamless transit across customs hubs in Europe, the Americas, and Asia.

Flexible Specifications

We blend coco peat, coco chips, and coco fiber ratios in custom percentages depending on your botanical crops. We deliver washed, unwashed, or calcium-buffered substrates to match specific plant rooting parameters.

Long-Term Partnerships

We believe in building sustainable business relationships. We support verified distributors with flexible credit policies, supply chain continuity guarantees, and direct technical consultations for wholesale growers.

MOQ & Payment

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for international shipments?

Our standard export minimum order quantity is 1 × 20ft FCL (Full Container Load) containing approximately 11 to 13 Metric Tons depending on product compression. For 40ft High Cube containers, the capacity ranges between 24 to 26 Metric Tons.

We also accommodate mixed-product containers (e.g., combining 5kg blocks, grow bags, and husk chips in a single shipment) to optimize buyer inventory.

Products & Specs

What technical specifications (EC, pH, expansion ratio, and sieve size) do your coir substrates meet?

Our coir substrates are engineered to meet strict international horticultural standards:

Electrical Conductivity (EC): Low EC washed grade (< 0.5 mS/cm tested via 1:1.5 extraction) & High EC unwashed grade.
pH Level: Balanced 5.5 – 6.8 range optimal for nutrient uptake in commercial crops.
Expansion Ratio: High volumetric yield of 80 to 85 Litres per 5kg compressed block.
Sieve Particle Size: Sieved to < 10% fiber and sand content (< 2%) for maximum water holding capacity & aeration.
Shipping & Logistics

Which ports do you export from and what are typical transit lead times?

We export directly from prime South Indian sea ports including Tuticorin (VOC Port), Chennai Port, and Cochin Port.

Average transit lead times after container loading:

Middle East & Gulf: 7 – 12 Days
Europe & UK: 18 – 25 Days
North America (East/West Coast): 25 – 35 Days
Australia & Far East Asia: 14 – 22 Days

Products & Specs

How do your Grow Bag Slabs and Coco Husk Chip blends optimize root aeration and moisture retention?

Our commercial Grow Bags and Husk Chips are engineered for high-yield greenhouse hydroponics:

Customized Blends: Tailored Coco Peat to Husk Chip ratios (e.g. 70:30 or 50:50) to optimize Air-Filled Porosity (AFP 18–25%) and WHC.
UV-Stabilized Polybag Slabs: Heavy-duty co-extruded white/black polybags (2–3 years UV guaranteed) with pre-cut plant, drip & drainage holes.
Uniform Chip Grading: Screened to 8–12mm, 12–18mm, or 18–22mm sizes, preventing compaction & promoting root branching.
Certifications & Quality

What export certifications and phytosanitary documents are provided?

Every export shipment from Anuradha Coirs is strictly inspected and accompanied by full official documentation:

Government Phytosanitary Certificate
Fumigation Certificate (Methyl Bromide / Heat Treated)
Certificate of Origin (COO / Coir Board)
Laboratory Test Report (EC, pH, Moisture & Sand %)
Bulk Ordering & Import

How do I import Coco Peat and Coir products in bulk?

Importing Coco Peat in bulk with Anuradha Coirs is simple and fully supported step-by-step:

1. Specify Requirements: Select your required products (5kg blocks, grow bags, bricks, Low/High EC, and required quantity).

2. Receive Quotation & Samples: We provide competitive FOB/CIF pricing along with product samples for quality testing.

3. Order Confirmation & Processing: Upon deposit or L/C confirmation, we compress, inspect, and package your shipment.

4. Customs Documentation & Dispatch: We handle loading into 20ft/40ft HC containers, phytosanitary certificates, fumigation, and COO for smooth customs clearance at your destination port.
