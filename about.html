Our History

Nearly a Decade of Excellence

Founded in 2016, Anuradha Coir and Fibres has grown into a trusted name in the manufacturing and supply of premium coco peat products. Established with a vision to provide sustainable and high-quality coir-based solutions to the horticulture and agriculture industries, the company has successfully completed nearly a decade of excellence in the coir manufacturing sector.

Under the leadership of Founder A. Aathi Ganesan, Director M. Karthikeyan, and Co-Director A. Anu Radha, Anuradha Coir and Fibres has continuously focused on quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction. Their dedication and expertise have helped the company build a strong reputation both in domestic and international markets.

Over the past 10 years, we have specialized in the manufacturing of a wide range of coco peat products, including 5 kg coco peat blocks, coco peat bricks, coco peat chips, coco coir coins/discs, and cocopeat grow bag slabs. Our products are carefully processed from natural coconut coir to ensure superior water retention, aeration, and eco-friendly performance for modern farming and gardening applications.

Today, Anuradha Coir and Fibres stands as a symbol of reliability and sustainability, committed to delivering world-class coco peat products while supporting greener and more productive agricultural practices.
